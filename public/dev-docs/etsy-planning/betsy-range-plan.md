# Etsy Shop — Product Range Plan (v1)

Built from a 15-listing sample across 7 UK shops, gathered 14 Aug 2026. All
competitor prices below are observed, not estimated. Cost and margin columns
are blank for Richard to complete.

**Companion document:** `etsy-shop-project-briefing.md` (origin, IP constraints).

---

## 1. Positioning

**Price at the market-clearing point, not premium.** The sample is unambiguous:

| Shop | Large board price | Shop sales |
|---|---|---|
| DIYPaperPrints | £54.89 A1 (3mm) / £57.29 (5mm) | **74.7k** |
| InkyStationery | £56.25 A1 (3mm) | **21k** |
| CustomWeddingPrints | £50.39 A1 (3mm) | **9.8k** |
| LacunaDesignOnline | £41.99 A2 on board (no A1) | **8.7k** |
| TheWriteGirlShop | £79–90 A1 display board | **5.9k** |
| InkiPinkiWeddings | £80 (800mm square board) | **2.2k** |
| thebellapaperieco | £120 A1 (5mm) | **86** |

The correlation is close to monotonic: everything priced £42–57 sits between
8.7k and 74.7k sales; everything priced £79+ sits between 86 and 5.9k. The
£120 shop has 86 sales in 2.5 years. **Do not position premium.**

**Differentiate on speed, not price.** Observed dispatch times:

- DIYPaperPrints, TheWriteGirlShop — 1–3 business days
- InkiPinkiWeddings — ~5 days
- thebellapaperieco — 48h proof + 7–10 days

The slow shops are slow *because of the dropship handoff*. We don't have one.
**"Printed in our own Norfolk workshop — dispatched within 48 hours"** is a
claim no reseller can match, in a category where buyers routinely order late.

**Secondary differentiators:** size range beyond A0 (large-format capability),
bespoke sizes (InkiPinkiWeddings sells an 800mm square board — buyers want
non-A-series), and genuine stock control.

## 1b. The shop-level price card (structural finding)

Every multi-listing shop in the sample applies **one fixed price ladder across
its entire catalogue**. Price is a property of the shop, not the product.

| Shop | Ladder (identical on every design sampled) |
|---|---|
| DIYPaperPrints | PDF £7.20 (celebration) / £14.40 (wedding) · Poster £18.99–24.29 · Canvas £31.05–68.31 · Foam 3mm £26.50–54.89 · Foam 5mm £34.89–57.29 |
| LacunaDesignOnline | A4 £10.99 · A3 £15.99 · A2 £26.99 · +backing board £21.99 / £26.99 / £41.99 |
| InkyStationery | A2 £48.75 · A1 £56.25 · 2 boards £78.75–90 · 3 boards £101.25–112.50 |
| TheWriteGirlShop | PDF £22 · Fine art A4 £35 / A3 £45 / A2 £50 / A1 £55 · Board A4 £45 / A3 £50 / A2 £60 / A1 £79–90 |
| InkiPinkiWeddings | 800mm board £80 · Poster £75 · Digital £40 · +Welcome board £144 |
| thebellapaperieco | Digital £32 · A2 £90 · A1 £120 · A0 £175 |

Verified across unrelated products — LacunaDesignOnline prices a bridal shower
sign and an "in loving memory" sign identically; InkyStationery prices a
three-board bundle and a single order-of-the-day sign off the same grid.

**Implication for us:** set one price card for the whole shop — a grid of
substrate × size × occasion band — and apply it to every product. Repricing then
becomes a single decision propagated across the range, rather than a per-product
negotiation. It also makes the range far quicker to extend: a new design inherits
its pricing automatically.

## 1c. Personalisation is where the margin is

Within a single shop (TheWriteGirlShop), on comparable physical items:

- "Love is Sweet" 8×10", **not personalised** — £12.00
- Signature Drinks A4, **personalised** — £35.00

Roughly 3× for the same piece of card. Their aisle set — ten 8×10" prints,
unpersonalised — is £29.99, under £3 a sheet.

Personalisation carries the price *and* the admin cost. That tension should
drive the range mix deliberately: personalised items for margin, unpersonalised
sets for throughput.

## 2. Design families

Five families, derived from the recurring aesthetics across ~130 sampled listings.

| # | Family | Description | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Minimalist Mono** | White ground, soft-black type, clean sans/serif mix | **Volume driver — build first** |
| 2 | **Eucalyptus Greenery** | Watercolour eucalyptus, sage/forest palette | Proven perennial |
| 3 | **Blush Floral** | Roses, hydrangea, peony; blush/ivory/pastel | Seasonal spring/summer |
| 4 | **Classic Script** | Calligraphy, ivory + warm gold | Traditional buyer |
| 5 | **Bow** | Satin bow motif, pink or blue | Baby/bridal shower trend |

All artwork original or built from licensed commercial-use assets. Families 1–4
carry the wedding range; family 5 carries showers.

**Excluded:** licensed characters (Winnie the Pooh, Peter Rabbit et al). The
substitutes — *classic honey-bear / woodland nursery* and *spring bunny /
garden* — reach the same buyer with no rights exposure.

## 6. Launch phases

Etsy listing fee is ~£0.16 per listing on a 4-month cycle — a 60-listing range
costs about £10 per cycle. Listing count is not the constraint; **artwork and
proofing capacity are.**

**Phase 1 — Launch (~15 listings)**
Minimalist Mono + Eucalyptus × (Welcome, Seating, Order of day) = 6
· Duo + Trio packs in both families = 4
· Engagement + Baby shower + Bridal shower in Minimalist = 3
· Aisle set + 1 reception small = 2

**Phase 2 — Depth (~14)**
Blush Floral + Classic Script core trio = 6 · packs = 4 · Bow family showers = 4

**Phase 3 — Breadth (~16)**
Full reception smalls range across families · gender reveal · bespoke-size listings

**Phase 4 — Evergreen (~8)**
Décor trios, framed and unframed

## 7. Open decisions

1. **Canvas capability.** Hand-stretching 280gsm canvas over 18mm pine is a
   different process from flatbed/large-format printing. In-house, outsourced,
   or dropped? Canvas is ~25% of competitor variants, so dropping it is a real
   loss — but not fatal.
2. **Digital PDF variant.** Stated position is no digital. Commercially I'd
   still include it: Etsy shows the *lowest* variant price in search, so
   competitors display "£7.20+" and upsell to a £50 board. Listing at "£51.95+"
   against "£7.20+" is a significant click-through disadvantage. Zero
   fulfilment cost. **Recommend reversing this — as a price anchor, not a product.**
3. **Brand identity.** Distinct from Event Stuff / Designs on Print, or linked?
4. **Easel upsell.** Every competitor excludes the easel and says so. Nobody
   appears to sell one alongside. Obvious attach.
5. **Frame upsell.** Doubles order value on décor trios (£52.19 → £100.79).
6. **Bespoke sizes.** Large format lets us quote any size; competitors offer
   this only via "message me". A structured bespoke-size listing could be a
   differentiator.

## 8. Operating notes

Practical observations from the sample that shape how the shop should run.

- **One listing carries the whole matrix.** Competitors put every size and
  substrate combination into a single listing as variations, rather than
  creating a separate listing per permutation. One listing fee instead of
  sixteen, and one place to edit.
- **Proofing is universal on personalised items** — a 48-hour proof is the
  market norm. Tiers 3 and 5 avoid it entirely, which is a strong argument for
  weighting the range toward them: margin per unit of admin is far better.
- **Personalisation capture is mixed.** Competitors take text fields for
  names, date and venue, plus a *file upload* for seating lists. Plan for both.
- **Keep tracking the competition.** Shop sales totals moved measurably between
  the two sampling passes: DIYPaperPrints 74.5k → 74.7k, InkyStationery
  20.9k → 21k, TheWriteGirlShop 5.8k → 5.9k, thebellapaperieco 81 → 86.
  Re-capturing the same shops periodically therefore yields a **sales-rate
  estimate** — the closest proxy to real bestseller data obtainable without
  eRank or Alura. A weekly note of each shop's sales total, in a spreadsheet,
  is enough. Caveat: totals are rounded to three significant figures, so single
  readings are noisy and the signal only firms up over several weeks.
- **Signals visible on any listing**, worth recording alongside the sales
  total: Bestseller badge, views in last 24 hours, in-baskets count, review
  count, and listing date.
